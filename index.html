How Refer & Earn Works
  1. Share your referral link or referral code with your friends.
  2. Your friend must use your referral link or enter your referral code during signup.
  3. Once registered successfully, the referral will reflect in your dashboard referral count.
  4. When your referred friend makes a qualifying deposit, you earn your referral reward.
  5. Your reward is added to your referral balance automatically.
Note: Your friend must use your referral link or enter your referral code during signup for the referral to be tracked.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why doesn't Global Digits use an automatic payment gateway?
Global Digits currently uses secure manual bank transfers to ensure payments reach us immediately without payment gateway settlement delays. Once you complete your transfer and submit your payment confirmation, our team verifies the payment and credits your wallet as quickly as possible. This system helps us process orders faster, maintain competitive pricing, and provide reliable service while keeping transactions secure.
