Frequently Asked Questions
What is an eSIM?
An eSIM is a digital SIM built into your device — no physical card needed. You activate it by scanning a QR code or entering an activation code we send you after purchase.
Is my phone compatible?
Most phones from 2018 onward support eSIM (iPhone XS and later, most Samsung Galaxy S/Note/Fold models from S20+, Google Pixel 3+). Check your device settings under "Mobile/Cellular Data" for an "Add eSIM" option to confirm before purchasing.
How long does delivery take?
eSIM delivery is currently handled manually by our team. Your order will show as "Pending" until we send your activation details, then automatically update to "Delivered" — you'll get a notification the moment it's ready.
Can I use my existing SIM at the same time?
Yes — eSIMs work alongside your physical SIM on dual-SIM capable devices, so you can keep your regular number active for calls/texts while using the eSIM for data.
