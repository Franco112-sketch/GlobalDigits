This check does not verify your legal identity. It only confirms that a live person is using the camera.
  • We use your camera to follow a few head movements (look up, down, left and right). Face tracking runs on your device.

Get ready

Allow camera access, then place your face inside the oval.

  • Use even lighting, with no strong light behind you.

  • Move your head, not just your eyes, and hold each movement briefly.
